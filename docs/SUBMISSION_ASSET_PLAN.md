## Screenshot quality bar

- Use the production URL, not localhost.
- Reset browser zoom to 100% and avoid OS scaling artifacts.
- Capture no personal tabs, account menus, notifications, or browser extensions.
- Keep the same viewport and crop across all five frames.
- Ensure small policy, grant, receipt, and denial text is legible at gallery size.
- Prefer frames that show cause and effect together instead of isolated close-ups.
